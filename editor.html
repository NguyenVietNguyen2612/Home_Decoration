User Guide

1. Add Objects: Drag and drop from the left library into the 2D or 3D view. Wallpapers and floors automatically apply to walls and ground.

2. Select & Transform: Left-click to select. Use the toolbar or press T (Move), E (Rotate), R (Scale). Drag the 3D Gizmo axes to adjust.

3. Properties Panel: When an object is selected, precisely edit its exact Position, Rotation, and Scale values on the right panel.

4. Undo / Redo: Use the toolbar arrows or press Ctrl+Z (Undo) and Ctrl+Y (Redo) to revert changes.

5. Camera Navigation: Right-click and drag to rotate (in 3D) or pan (in 2D). Use the Scroll wheel to Zoom In/Out.

6. Environment: Adjust the ☀️ Time of Day slider to change lighting, and tweak 📐 Grid Settings for precision snapping.

7. Delete Objects: Select objects and press Delete / Backspace or the 🗑️ Delete button on the toolbar.

8. Project Actions: Use New Room to start fresh, Save to download your .json project, and Preview for a cinematic view.
